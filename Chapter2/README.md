## Supplementary Table A: Extracted Data

This table provides a comprehensive summary of the data extracted from studies included in our systematic review. It covers key aspects such as publication year, authors, study purpose, design, sampling methods, types of food business operations examined, and factors extracted for analysis. These factors have been grouped categorically for clarity. Additionally, the table details the indicators and outcome measures utilized across studies, alongside the results of the studies. This table serves as the foundation for our analysis.

## Supplementary Table B: Factor List

Table B categorizes and lists the factors extracted from the reviewed studies, organized into three primary groups for ease of understanding. Each group is further divided into subgroups, with the factors presented alongside their further explanations and the targeted food business operations. The number of studies examining each factor, and references to these studies are also included. This table provides a structured framework for the factor list found through our research.
